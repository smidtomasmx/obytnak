Rates

Rental price per day

The price depends on the season in which the rental starts and ends. Rental length is counted in days (both the pick-up day and the return day count). Minimum rental length is 5 days in high season, otherwise 2 days.

Prices below are shown in euros as an approximate conversion (≈ 25 CZK/€) for your convenience – the Czech crown amount in brackets is the binding one, since the contract, invoices and deposit are all handled in CZK on a Czech bank account.
SeasonPeriodMin. lengthPrice
High season July – August 5 days €156 / day (3 900 Kč)
Shoulder season June, September 2 days €132 / day (3 300 Kč)
Low season January – May, October – December 2 days €104 / day (2 600 Kč)
Service fee one-off – €60 (1 500 Kč)
Deposit (refundable) one-off – €1 000 (25 000 Kč)

Discounts for longer rentals: 10% off from 11 days, 15% off from 21 days. Discounts don't stack and only apply to the rental fee. The price is calculated by the number of days. A one-off service fee of €60 (1 500 Kč) is charged on every rental. The rental includes unlimited mileage, insurance, the motorway vignette and basic equipment. All prices include VAT.

Prices are indicative. The exact price is calculated by the calculator on the Booking page.

Terms

Good to know

Pick-up and return

  • Pick-up from 7:00 am, return by 5:00 pm
  • We hand over the van clean with a full tank – please return it in the same condition
  • The driver must hold a valid category B licence and be at least 21 years old
  • A detailed walk-through of the van is part of the hand-over

Deposit and insurance

  • Refundable deposit of €1 000 (25 000 Kč) at pick-up
  • The vehicle is insured (liability, comprehensive cover, EU-wide assistance)
  • The booking is confirmed once the deposit is paid
  • Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure

Mileage

  • No mileage limit, we don't charge for kilometres driven
  • The Czech motorway vignette is included in the price

Rules on board

  • Smoking is prohibited throughout the van
  • Pets are strictly not allowed
  • Only empty the toilet and waste water at designated disposal points
