About the van

Space and comfort for the whole crew

The Carado A 464 is a 2023 over-cab motorhome built on a Fiat Ducato chassis. Thanks to its large over-cab bed, fixed rear bed and convertible dining area, up to six people can sleep comfortably.

At the back you'll find a fixed bed, in the middle a fully equipped kitchen, and a bathroom with a separate shower and toilet. It can be driven with a category B licence and is also suitable for families with children (ISOFIX for two car seats).

Dimensions: 7.24 m long
